# SIMBIOCHEM II @ NeurIPS 2026 — Call for Papers (fetched 2026-09-04)

- Submission deadline: 04 September 2026 (AoE)
- Decisions: 28 September 2026. Workshop: 11-12 December 2026, NeurIPS, Sydney, Australia.
- Page limit: 5-8 pages, EXCLUDING references, appendices and data-availability statements.
- Template: official NeurIPS 2026 LaTeX style; omit `final` and `preprint` options.
- Anonymisation REQUIRED: no author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, identifying links,
  GitHub repos, personal websites, or identifying PDF metadata.
- Archival status: NON-ARCHIVAL. No proceedings, no rebuttal phase. Work previously presented
  or under review elsewhere is welcome. Camera-ready collated on a website proceedings page (opt-out available).
- Review: double-blind, up to three reviewers per submission, capped at three papers per reviewer.
- SCORING CRITERIA: Novelty, Clarity, and Impact, each 1-5, plus detailed comments.
- No rebuttal phase -> the submitted PDF must stand entirely on its own.

## Topics of interest
- Physically-grounded architectures (symmetries, conservation laws)
- Differentiable simulation & inverse design
- Next-generation learned potentials
- Data efficiency, scalability, foundation models, agentic ML
- Standardisation, reproducibility, benchmarks, datasets, metrics
