Model & data notes +

The model. Senba adapts MarS-FM to fixed-lag transitions from mdCATH at 450 K. Samples are independent endpoints. The sample index identifies a draw, and playback speed controls display time.

The evidence. The included examples are saved predictions on a previously used validation domain. Fresh runs execute the released checkpoint on your local CPU. Custom inputs are outside that specific validation panel.

The view. Backbone curves follow real Cα coordinates. The atom view shows heavy atoms when available. Input-to-endpoint morphs linearly interpolate displayed coordinates. The MD movie shows chronologically recorded simulation frames.
