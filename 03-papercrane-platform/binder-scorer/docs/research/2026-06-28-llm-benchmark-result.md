# (v1) First Benchmark Result (2026-06-28)

**TL;DR:** The literature-informed, signed, per-target LLM prior (`B_LLM`, generated by
`claude-opus-4-6` over PubMed + Open Targets + Orbion biology menu) **does NOT beat raw
within-fold min_ipSAE** on the 11-target LOTO scoreboard. Mean ΔPrecision@k = **−0.058**
vs B0, and it loses to B5/B6 too. ~~The result is adequately powered (|Δ| > pre-registered
MDE 0.035), so this is a real loss, not an underpowered "untested."~~ **[RETRACTED 2026-07-01 —
see correction banner: the real MDE is ≈0.150, so −0.058 is UNTESTED, not a powered loss.]**
**Verdict across all three comparisons: FALL_BACK_TO_GLOBAL** (raw win-count verdict; NOT
power-supported).

## Numbers (candidate = B_LLM, on `labeled_features_bio.csv`, 11 LOTO folds)

| Baseline | mean ΔP@k | 95% BCa CI | wins | recall ΔCI | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B0 (raw within-fold min_ipSAE) | −0.058 | [−0.144, 0.062] | 2/11 | [−0.104, 0.034] | fall_back_to_global |
| B6 (global LightGBM)           | −0.037 | [−0.119, 0.041] | 3/11 | [−0.105, −0.015] | fall_back_to_global |
| B5 (target-cond. LightGBM)     | −0.038 | [−0.161, 0.112] | 4/11 | [−0.109, 0.073] | fall_back_to_global |

Per-target ΔP@k vs **B0**: EGFR −0.10, FGFR2 −0.20, IL7R −0.05, INSULINR +0.10,
MDM2 −0.25, MZB1 **+0.33**, NIPAH 0.00*, PD1 0.00, PDGFRB −0.17, PDL1 0.00, PMVK −0.30.
\*NIPAH has no prior (model safety-refused the viral target) → B0 fallback, so its
deltas vs B6/B5 (+0.20/+0.25) are the *fallback* winning, not the LLM.

## Root cause (from `llm_priors.json` weight analysis)

1. **Signal dilution.** B0 = 100% weight on min_ipSAE (pooled AUC 0.806 — the dominant
   metric). The LLM placed only **~16%** of L1 weight on ipSAE and spread the rest across
   26 metrics, including **~30% on the 9 weak/noisy `bio__` features**. Moving weight off
   the best metric onto worse ones reduces ranking quality — mechanically guaranteed.
2. **Near target-invariant prior.** ipSAE weight = 0.15–0.17 and bio weight = 0.29–0.34
   for *every* target. The "per-target" prior barely differentiates by target. The only
   genuine per-target signals were the MDM2 `topology_strand_frac` SIGN-FLIP (−0.021 vs
   +0.024…+0.038 elsewhere — the predicted Orbion architecture flip) and the per-target
   epitope-residue lists / liabilities / citations.

## What worked (keep)
- Pipeline runs end-to-end: PubMed+OpenTargets retrieval → literature-informed prior →
  signed L1-normalized weights → leakage-safe weighted-sum scorer → LOTO harness.
- Priors carry real, varying epitope residues, liabilities, and PMID citations per target.
- The Orbion sign-flip showed up exactly once (MDM2 strand), validating that *signed*
  per-target weighting is at least expressible.
- 10/11 priors generated; NIPAH cleanly handled via B0 fallback (model safety refusal).

## What to fix (v2 hypotheses, in priority order)
1. **Tell the LLM ipSAE empirically dominates** and instruct it to CONCENTRATE weight on
   the top structural metric unless literature gives a strong reason to deviate (give it
   the per-metric AUCs from `reference_metric_thresholds`). A diluted prior cannot beat a
   single strong metric.
2. **Force per-target differentiation** — the v1 prior is nearly uniform. Either prompt for
   sparsity (pick ≤5 metrics that matter for THIS target) or penalize uniformity.
3. **Demote / drop the bio block** in the weighted sum until there's evidence it carries
   signal — 30% on bio is unearned. Test bio as a *data-weighted* arm (B6 + bio columns)
   to see if DATA can extract any bio signal before letting the LLM weight it.
4. **Per-target headroom is real** (MZB1 +0.33, INSULINR +0.10) — the loss is an *average*
   over a high-variance set, not a uniform failure. A sharper prior could flip the mean.

## Honest caveats
- n=11 targets: every CI crosses or sits near 0; "powered" here means |Δ| > MDE 0.035, not
  a tight estimate. Treat magnitudes as directional.
- K5 memorization arm NOT run — famous targets (EGFR/PDL1) could inflate apparent LLM skill;
  here the LLM *lost*, so memorization isn't masking a win, but a novel-target control is
  still the honest follow-up.
- `failure_penalties` from the live priors are stored as named floats, not feature-threshold
  rules, so they were skipped by the scorer (signed weights carried the signal).
- Dynamics features excluded by design.
