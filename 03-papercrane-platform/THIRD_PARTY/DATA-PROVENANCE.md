# Binder-scorer data provenance and redistribution status

This inventory identifies the bundled data as inspected on 2026-11-01. It records sources and the dataset-specific terms that require confirmation before redistribution. Software licenses, model-weight terms, dataset licenses and individual article rights are separate. The historical research inventory's “commercial-OK” and “validation-only” labels are planning judgments, not grants. Original data and historical labels have not been silently rewritten.
