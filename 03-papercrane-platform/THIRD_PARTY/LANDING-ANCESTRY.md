# Landing page source and origami ancestry

The vendored landing application is from [`vkalipat/papercrane-website`](https://github.com/vkalipat/papercrane-website) at commit `ca760d7a8fa82044bb1f4cac3549dc9b7b46a455`, as recorded in [UPSTREAM.md](../apps/web/landing/UPSTREAM.md). The local original repository identifies the commit author as `pikachuandme1`. This is contributor provenance, not proof of sole ownership. That revision has no standalone LICENSE/NOTICE and no author/license field in its root package manifest. A broader copyright grant from the co-founder's website cannot be inferred from the portfolio's MIT license.

Files in `apps/web/landing/src/lib/origami-runtime/` retain their comments identifying ports from [`georgiee/origami`](https://github.com/georgiee/origami). The upstream notice at `42e5e27fec997bb9d5d754fc5b3d3382bdb98704` credits **Georgios Kaleadis, 2026**, under MIT. Its unmodified text is bundled in [georgiee-origami-MIT-42e5e27.txt](licenses/georgiee-origami-MIT-42e5e27.txt).

That origami implementation has earlier ancestry in **Origami Editor 3D**, by **Attila Bágyoni**. The examined upstream source at [`bagyoni/origamieditor3d@13b0a8cefe37b467ca2ada1e6dd83ab17cce8688`](https://github.com/bagyoni/origamieditor3d/tree/13b0a8cefe37b467ca2ada1e6dd83ab17cce8688) includes `OrigamiGen1.java` with the author's attribution. The corresponding [GPL-3.0 text](licenses/origamieditor3d-GPL-3.0.txt) is preserved here alongside the later MIT notice.

These notices record the source chain. They do not assert that a later MIT notice removed obligations attached to earlier derived material, that the combined landing application is MIT-cleared, or that the applicant owns all frontend code. The portfolio's root MIT grant applies only to original material its copyright holder can license and does not relicense this combined third-party material. The source files and their existing credits remain available for review.
