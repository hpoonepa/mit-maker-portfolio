The upstream notice at `42e5e27fec997bb9d5d754fc5b3d3382bdb98704` credits **Georgios Kaleadis, 2026**, under MIT. Its unmodified text is bundled in [georgiee-origami-MIT-42e5e27.txt](licenses/georgiee-origami-MIT-42e5e27.txt).

The examined upstream source at [`bagyoni/origamieditor3d@13b0a8cefe37b467ca2ada1e6dd83ab17cce8688`](https://github.com/bagyoni/origamieditor3d/tree/13b0a8cefe37b467ca2ada1e6dd83ab17cce8688) includes `OrigamiGen1.java` with the author's attribution. The corresponding [GPL-3.0 text](licenses/origamieditor3d-GPL-3.0.txt) is preserved here alongside the later MIT notice.

These notices record the source chain and its contributors. Earlier license obligations remain relevant to derived material; permission for the combined landing application requires review.
