# Billing

Paper Crane is billed in **seeds**. One seed roughly equals one design through one
standard tool node. Plans bundle seeds, concurrency, and retention.

## Plans

| Plan         | Monthly | Seeds / mo | Concurrent jobs | Retention | Frontier |
|--------------|---------|------------|------------------|-----------|----------|
| Free         | $0      | 50         | 2                | 30 days   | no       |
| Researcher   | $49     | 2,500      | 4                | 90 days   | yes      |
| Lab          | $499    | 30,000     | 16               | 1 year    | yes      |
| Institute    | $2,499  | 200,000    | 64               | indefinite| yes      |
| Enterprise   | custom  | custom     | custom           | custom    | custom   |

Plans are billed monthly or annually (15% discount on annual).

## Seed cost reference

| Tool tier  | Tools                                          | Seeds / design |
|------------|------------------------------------------------|----------------|
| Cheap      | ESM-2, ProteinMPNN, SolubleMPNN, LigandMPNN    | 0.1            |
| Standard   | RFdiffusion, Chroma, BoltzGen, Boltz-1         | 1              |
| Heavy      | BindCraft, Boltz-2, Chai-1                     | 1              |
| Frontier   | AlphaFold 3                                    | 2              |

A typical default-pipeline run (200 backbones × 3 sequences × Boltz-2 validate ≈ 600
designs) costs **~720 seeds**.

## Overage

Overage charges only apply if you opt in (off by default). When enabled:

| Plan         | Overage rate (per seed) |
|--------------|--------------------------|
| Researcher   | $0.025                   |
| Lab          | $0.020                   |
| Institute    | $0.015                   |

Overage is billed in arrears at the end of each period. We email you at 80% of plan
allowance and again at 100%.

## Top-ups

Don't want overage but need more seeds this month? Buy a top-up:

| Bundle    | Price   | Effective rate |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| 1,000     | $30     | $0.030         |
| 10,000    | $250    | $0.025         |
| 100,000   | $2,000  | $0.020         |

Top-up seeds **never expire**. They're only consumed after your plan seeds run out, so
buying ahead is safe.

## Refunds

We refund seeds automatically when:

- A job fails after exhausting all retries (full refund of unstarted nodes)
- Our infra causes the failure (full refund regardless of node state)
- A frontier tool is unavailable for > 30 minutes (full refund + credit)

## Cancellations

Cancel any time from **Settings → Billing**. You retain access through the end of the
paid period, then drop to the free tier (your designs and projects remain — only the
seed allowance changes).

## Invoices & VAT

Invoices are generated on the first of each month (or on charge for top-ups). EU/UK
customers are charged with reverse-charge VAT — supply your VAT number under
**Settings → Billing → Tax** to remove the line item.

<Callout variant="tip">
On the free tier and want to upgrade? You don't lose your projects, jobs, or designs —
the upgrade is instant and your next job picks up the new allowance immediately.
</Callout>
