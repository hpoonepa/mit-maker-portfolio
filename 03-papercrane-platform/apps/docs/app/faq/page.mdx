# FAQ

## How is Paper Crane different from running these tools myself?

You can absolutely run RFdiffusion, BindCraft, and Boltz-2 yourself. We've done it. The
gap isn't capability — it's **methodology**. We give you:

- One adapter spec across 12 tools so a pipeline definition is portable
- Reproducibility manifests (image digests, seeds, input hashes) on every design
- Validated metric thresholds from our 60-binder benchmark suite
- Auto-scaled GPU fleet so you don't queue locally

## What GPUs do you run on?

A mix of A10, A100-40GB, A100-80GB, and H100-80GB across Vast.ai and a small reserved
fleet. Tool manifests pin the required class — a job lands on the cheapest GPU that
meets the requirement.

## Do you train on my designs?

No. We do not use your input structures, sequences, or metric outputs to train any
model. Your data is encrypted at rest, isolated per-project, and deleted on schedule
when you cancel. Read the [privacy policy](https://papercrane.bio/privacy) for the
binding version of that statement.

## Can I run frontier tools (AlphaFold 3) on the free tier?

No. AlphaFold 3 is rate-limited and gated to paid tiers because of its license. Use
Boltz-2 or Chai-1 for free-tier validation — they're remarkably close to AF3 on
binder-benchmark tasks.

## How do you handle reproducibility across GPU non-determinism?

We pin tool image digests (`sha256:...`) so the binary is bit-exact. Where the upstream
tool exposes deterministic mode, we enable it by default. Where it doesn't (some CUDA
ops are non-deterministic regardless), the metric variation across replays is typically
< 0.5% on ipTM/pAE. The manifest records the runtime so you can compare apples-to-apples.

## Why "seeds"?

Two reasons. First, every tool exposes a random seed; "seed" is the unit of one
forward pass through one tool. Second, seeds are decoupled from GPU minutes — when we
move from A100 to H100, your bill doesn't change.

## Can I bring my own GPU?

Enterprise plans only. We provide a worker container that registers with our scheduler
and pulls jobs against your project's quota. Useful if you have on-prem infra and need
data residency.

## Do you support targets that aren't in PDB?

Yes. Upload a FASTA (we'll predict the structure with Boltz-2 first), or upload a
PDB/CIF directly. We also accept AlphaFold accessions that pull from EBI's AFDB.

## What happens to my designs if I cancel?

They stay accessible for 30 days at the free-tier retention. After that, we delete the
artifacts (CIF/PDB/metric JSON). The metadata (counts, hashes) is retained for audit
log integrity. You can export everything via `papercrane projects export` before you
cancel — bundles are zip files of the raw artifacts.

## How do I report a bug or weird metric?

`hello@papercrane.bio` — please include the design ID (`dsn_...`) and a brief
description. We respond within one business day. For security issues, use
`security@papercrane.bio` and we'll respond within four hours.
