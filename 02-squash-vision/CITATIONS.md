# SquashVision citations

SquashVision integrates pretrained models and open-source tools. My work covers the application, review workflow, calibration, tracking integration and heuristic shot rubric. The pretrained models are credited below. Versions below come from the checked-in lockfiles; source links were reviewed on 1 November 2026.

## Models and research

- **Ultralytics YOLO11 pose.** Ultralytics, [YOLO11 documentation](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yolo11/) and [source, v8.4.129](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/tree/v8.4.129). Player pose uses `yolo11n-pose.pt` from the [Ultralytics assets v8.3.0 release](https://github.com/ultralytics/assets/releases/tag/v8.3.0). Package and checkpoint versions are distinct. The model registry and loader record the expected bytes and hash. [Upstream license](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/blob/v8.4.129/LICENSE): AGPL-3.0; [Ultralytics licensing options](https://www.ultralytics.com/license).
- **Adapted ball model implementation.** Linfeng Dong et al., [RacketVision: A Multiple Racket Sports Benchmark for Unified Ball and Racket Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17045v3), AAAI 2026. The local inference module adapts [OrcustD/RacketVision `source/BallTrack/model/tracknet_v3.py`](https://github.com/OrcustD/RacketVision/blob/c44af2a08524d3cb54d818f19686f4cdea4d2793/source/BallTrack/model/tracknet_v3.py) at commit `c44af2a08524d3cb54d818f19686f4cdea4d2793`. The exact [pinned source license](https://github.com/OrcustD/RacketVision/blob/c44af2a08524d3cb54d818f19686f4cdea4d2793/LICENSE) is MIT, copyright 2025 Linfeng Dong. The [local attribution](src/squashvision/backend/squashvision/vision/ball/ATTRIBUTION.txt) preserves that notice and lists adaptations.
- **TrackNetV3 research lineage.** Yu-Jou Chen and Yu-Shuen Wang, *TrackNetV3: Enhancing ShuttleCock Tracking with Augmentations and Trajectory Rectification*, ACM Multimedia Asia 2023, [doi:10.1145/3595916.3626370](https://doi.org/10.1145/3595916.3626370); [authors' paper](https://people.cs.nycu.edu.tw/~yushuen/data/TrackNetV3.pdf) and [original implementation](https://github.com/qaz812345/TrackNetV3). SquashVision uses the RacketVision adaptation above; it does not implement the entire original training/trajectory-inpainting system.
- **Ball checkpoint.** [`linfeng302/RacketVision-Models/checkpoints/balltrack_best.pth`](https://huggingface.co/linfeng302/RacketVision-Models/blob/a3760773233a0988c9605259743fbdd87c59d3a3/checkpoints/balltrack_best.pth), revision `a3760773233a0988c9605259743fbdd87c59d3a3`; SHA-256 `00d707b9db7a49561c411e4765956e79bcd7c7e20c7a0a535073440b3e972342`, 46,585,585 bytes. The [upstream downloader](https://github.com/OrcustD/RacketVision/blob/c44af2a08524d3cb54d818f19686f4cdea4d2793/source/download_checkpoints.py) identifies this model repository. It currently has [no model card](https://huggingface.co/linfeng302/RacketVision-Models), and a checkpoint-specific license was not established. The registry therefore keeps `not-declared`; the code's MIT license is not presented as permission for these weights.

## Court geometry and interface references

Court dimensions and line placement used in calibration are documented against World Squash's [Singles Rules 2020, version 2020-02, Appendix 7](https://worldsquashofficiating.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Rules-of-Singles-Squash-2020-V2.pdf). The implementation uses the court geometry in that edition.

The [original design notes](docs/design-notes.md) credit the local Lambert wiki and Papercrane work for layout ideas, and [Core Insights](https://coresportsai.com/core-insights), [RallyVision](https://rallyvision.ai/) and [PlaySight's app](https://www.playsight.tv/playsight-app) for match-review workflow inspiration. I used these as references for the match-review interface.
